7 · Banner, inline alert, toast

Banner: inside a view or panel, persists until resolved; warn and error take the matching ring (the tile rule). Inline alert: one line under a field or row. Toast: transient, bottom-right, elev-2. Errors are role="alert" and stay until dismissed; info and success leave after 5s (paused on hover or focus); max 3 shown, older collapse to "+n more", all kept in Notifications.

9 · Tabs, segmented control, list row, tree row

Segmented = a small mode choice on one surface (role="tablist" when it swaps a panel, "radiogroup" when it sets a value); it keeps the view switch. Tabs = documents (file tabs) and Settings sections, underline style, overflow into a "more" menu. List row and tree row share height, hover, selection and focus; trailing actions show on hover or focus-within and are always in the context menu, so nothing is hover-only.
